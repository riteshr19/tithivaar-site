# Tithivaar website

Static site for the Tithivaar Android app (Hindu Panchang & calendar), served at
https://tithivaar.riteshrana.engineer from Cloudflare Pages.

- `privacy.html`: privacy policy (the URL given to Google Play)
- `app-ads.txt`: authorised AdMob seller line
- `get/`: share-link target that sends Android visitors to the Play listing
- `blog/`, `index.html`: landing page and articles generated from the app's content
- `content/v1/`: signed calendar-data packs the app downloads

Pages are generated by `tools/build_site.py` in the app project. Deploy with
`wrangler pages deploy . --project-name=tithivaar --branch=main`.
