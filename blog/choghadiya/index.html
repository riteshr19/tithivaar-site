Understanding Choghadiya

Eight periods of day and night, and how they are read.

Choghadiya divides the time from sunrise to sunset into eight equal parts, and sunset to the next sunrise into eight more. Each part is roughly an hour and a half.

Each period carries a name, Amrit, Shubh, Labh, Char, Udveg, Kaal or Rog. Amrit, Shubh and Labh are traditionally favourable; Char is considered suitable for travel; Udveg, Kaal and Rog are usually avoided for new beginnings.

Because periods are measured from local sunrise and sunset, they change with the city and the season.
