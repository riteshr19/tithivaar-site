FAQ

Frequently asked.

Is Tithivaar really free?

Yes. Every feature is free. The app shows a few ads, always labelled “Ad”, and personalised ads stay off unless you turn them on.

Which cities does it support?

16 cities: Ahmedabad, Mumbai, New Delhi, Varanasi, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Dallas, Houston, New York, San Francisco, Toronto, London, Dubai, Singapore and Sydney. “Use current location” picks the nearest one.

Amanta or Purnimanta?

Both. Choose the month system your family follows in Settings. Festivals fall on the same day either way; only month names around the full moon differ.

Where do festival dates come from?

They follow a reference calendar (Drik Panchang, New Delhi) for September 2026 to August 2027, with vrat days computed for your city. Local observance can sometimes differ by a day.

Does it need the internet?

No. The Panchang is calculated on your phone. When online, the app fetches small, signed updates to festival and holiday data.

Is my data private?

There is no account and nothing about you is sent to us. Your settings and reminders stay on your phone. See the privacy policy.

Can I count japa from the lock screen?

Yes. Turn on the lock-screen counter in the mantra player, or add the Japa tile to Quick Settings. There's also a Japa widget for the home screen.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Tithivaar is on Android (Google Play) for now.
